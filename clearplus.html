The ClearPlus 5 Pillars of Digital Equity

ClearPlus is Aurora OS's built-in accessibility architecture. We believe accessibility is not an afterthought or a "special mode" — it is a core civil right. ClearPlus specialists co-navigate apps and websites with full conversational depth, technical accuracy, and zero condescension.

👁️

1. ClearSite — Screen Vision & Low-Vision Navigation

Specialist: Iris

Provides rich semantic audio descriptions of complex screens, charts, and diagrams. Features headless DOM tree narration, high-contrast layouts, and OCR document reading.

🌈

2. ClearSay — AAC & Assistive Speech Synthesis

Specialist: Clara

High-efficiency symbol communication boards and phrase composition engines for non-speaking communicators. Supports personalized vocal synthesis and instant chat integration.

🌿

3. ClearStep — Older Adults & Step-by-Step Guidance

Specialist: Sage

Calm, distraction-free companion that walks users through everyday tasks (banking, appointments, video calls) in plain English. Includes active scam shields and memory prompts.

🤟

4. ClearSign — Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing Parity

Specialist: Nova

Real-time visual captions for system audio, meetings, and videos. Identifies ambient environmental cues (doorbells, alarms, sirens) and ensures 100% visual notification parity.

🎯

5. ClearTouch — Motor & Switch Access

Specialist: Dex

Dwell selection, single/dual switch scanning, enlarged click targets, tremor-filtering algorithms, and full voice command navigation for physical independence.
