The ClearPlus 7-Agent Universal Access Suite

ClearPlus is the universal accessibility architecture powering both Aurora OS (www.aurora-system.ca) and Understanding Humans (www.understandinghumans.app). Every ClearPlus agent explicitly tells users who it is for and what it does—and the entire 7-agent suite is engineered as a drop-in SDK so other app companies can include ClearPlus directly inside their already-made apps.

📣

1. ClearSay — Non-Verbal AAC Board & Plain-Language Access

Specialist: Clara • SDK Hook: AacOverlayWidget + AacService
Who it is for: Non-speaking, minimally speaking, or situational speech-loss users (autism, aphasia, cerebral palsy, ALS, TBI, selective mutism, or acute stress), AAC symbol-board users, and anyone who wants short plain-language sentences.

What it does: Provides an always-reachable 50+ symbol communication board across 6 categories (Needs, Feelings, People, Actions, Questions, Medical/Crisis) with tap-to-compose phrase strips, text-to-speech output with visual fallback, and a plain-language guide mode that keeps sentences to 10–12 words with zero jargon or idioms.

💬

2. ClearSign — Deaf, Hard-of-Hearing & No-Voice Crisis Access

Specialist: Nova • SDK Hook: ClearSignOverlayWidget + ClearSignService
Who it is for: Deaf, hard-of-hearing, deafblind, and non-speaking phone users, plus ASL/LSQ-first users who prefer clear plain text over dense written English.

What it does: Places one-tap no-voice crisis routes on every screen—SMS text (Text 988, Text HOME to 741741, Text 911), web chat (988 Chat), 711 IP relay, and ASL videophone—plus a "This device has no phone line" filter for Wi-Fi-only tablets, one-tap plain-language switching, and a build-enforced guarantee that no feature in the app is sound-only.

👁️

3. ClearSite — Blind, Low-Vision & Voice-Control Access

Specialist: Iris • SDK Hook: ClearSiteOverlayWidget + ClearSiteScreenScanner
Who it is for: Blind, low-vision, and print-disabled users, or anyone who needs the screen read aloud and operated by spoken voice commands.

What it does: Scans the live semantics tree to announce every screen on arrival (putting crisis help first), reads every heading, button, and text field aloud with on-screen captions, executes spoken voice commands ("read the screen", "tap continue", "scroll down", "go back", "type message") with yes/no confirmation before destructive actions, and provides 7 on-device camera vision channels (text/OCR, product, currency, medicine, scene, colour, person).

🧓

4. ClearCare (ClearStep Senior) — Pre-Tap Button Reassurance, Scam Shield & Visual Comfort

Specialist: Sage • SDK Hook: SeniorOverlayWidget + ClearCarePanel + SeniorModeService
Who it is for: Older adults, cautious technology users, and anyone who wants reassurance about what a button does before clicking it, protection from scams, and easy controls for size, contrast, button/icon size, and spacing.

What it does:

  • Pre-Tap Button Explainer ("What Things Do Before You Click Them"): Scans the current screen and explains in warm, plain language what each button does before you tap it—reassuring you that it will not charge money, call anyone, or break your device.
  • Scam Checker & Trust Guides: Checks suspicious phone calls, emails, and texts against 15+ elder-fraud patterns (texted 6-digit bank codes, gift cards, AnyDesk/TeamViewer remote access, fake USPS/FedEx delivery fees, grandchild bail scams) and provides simple red-flag checklists for trusting phone calls, emails, and SMS texts.
  • Live Size, Contrast, Button/Icon Size & Spacing Controls: Instant adjustment for Text Size (1.0× to 1.65×), High-Contrast outlines & bold ink, Button & Icon Size (48dp, 56dp, 64dp), and Extra Spacing between rows and buttons.
👀

5. ClearGaze — Paralysis, Eye-Tracking Dwell-to-Click & Single-Switch Access

Specialist: Dex • SDK Hook: ClearGazeOverlayWidget + ClearGazeService + UhTappable
Who it is for: Paralyzed users (ALS, quadriplegia, spinal cord injury, brainstem stroke / locked-in syndrome, cerebral palsy) who operate their device using their eyes (eye-tracking gaze hardware) or a single blink, sip-and-puff, or head switch.

What it does:

  • Eye-Gaze Dwell-to-Click: Hold your gaze steady over any button for a chosen duration (0.8s, 1.2s, 1.8s, or 2.5s) to see a radial countdown ring and click automatically—zero finger or button press required.
  • Oversized Top Eye-Bar Dock: High-margin gaze targets for Scroll Up, Scroll Down, Go Back, and Rest Eyes (pauses dwell-clicking so you can read comfortably without accidental clicks).
  • Single-Switch Auto-Scanner & ClearStep 48×48dp Hit Targets: Automatically steps a highlight across every interactive control on the live screen for single-switch activation, backed by UhTappable 48×48dp minimum hit targets.
🫧

6. ClearTouch — Sensory Overload, Autism, Migraine & Motion-Reduction Access

Specialist: Calm • SDK Hook: ClearTouchOverlayWidget + ClearTouchService
Who it is for: Anyone experiencing sensory overload, motion sensitivity, migraine, TBI, autism, or visual overwhelm who needs the app turned down immediately without hunting through Settings.

What it does: Reduces sensory input one step at a time in a predictable order—(1) Stop the movement (disables all animations across the widget tree), (2) Soften the colours (activates sensory-safe palette), and (3) Strip it back to words (plain-text mode)—with one-tap undo ("Put the last one back" / "Put everything back") and zero diagnostic labels.

🧠

7. ClearFocus — Dyslexia, ADHD, Brain-Fog & Reading-Ruler Access

Specialist: Focus • SDK Hook: ClearFocusOverlayWidget + ClearFocusService
Who it is for: Users with dyslexia, ADHD, post-concussion / TBI reading fatigue, long-COVID or chemo brain fog, or executive dysfunction.

What it does: Provides a movable horizontal Reading Ruler (line-focus bar) that highlights 2–3 lines of text while shading distracting clutter above and below, widens letter spacing (+0.6px), word spacing (+2.0px), and line height (1.6×) across the entire app to prevent letter crowding, and shows a "Where Am I & What Is Next?" screen anchor.

🔌 For Other App Creators & Studios — Include ClearPlus in Already-Made Apps

Every ClearPlus agent is engineered to sit above the host app's router/navigator (such as Flutter's MaterialApp.builder or web DOM overlays) and read the live accessibility semantics tree automatically.

  • Zero Screen Rewrites: Drop all 7 ClearPlus agents—or choose individual agents like ClearCare, ClearGaze, or ClearSay—into an existing mobile or web app in minutes.
  • Self-Describing UI: Every ClearPlus panel includes a built-in banner telling end users Who it is for and What it does in plain, respectful language.
