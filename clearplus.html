The clearplus Package — Clara AA & 7 Stackable Modes

clearplus is the universal accessibility package powering Aurora OS (www.aurora-system.ca), Understanding Humans (www.understandinghumans.app), and Kinder Connect.

🌈 Clara is your single Accessibility Autonomous Agent (AA): No matter which ClearPlus mode or combination of modes you turn on, Clara is the one trusted voice who communicates with you, reads for you, and guides you—so you never have to re-learn a new persona when your needs change.

⚡ Simultaneous Multi-Mode Stacking (1 to 7 Modes at Once): All 7 Clear* tools are stackable modes under Clara. Users with overlapping needs can activate any combination at the same time (for example, ClearSay + ClearGaze + ClearTouch, or ClearSite + ClearCare + ClearFocus).

📣

1. ClearSay Mode — Non-Verbal AAC Board & Plain-Language Access

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: clearsay • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + AacOverlayWidget
Who it is for: Non-speaking, minimally speaking, or situational speech-loss users (autism, aphasia, cerebral palsy, ALS, TBI, selective mutism, or acute stress), AAC symbol-board users, and anyone who wants short plain-language sentences.

What Clara does in ClearSay mode: Provides an always-reachable 50+ symbol communication board across 6 categories (Needs, Feelings, People, Actions, Questions, Medical/Crisis) with tap-to-compose phrase strips, text-to-speech output with visual fallback, and a plain-language guide mode that keeps sentences to 10–12 words with zero jargon or idioms. Explore ClearSay Deep-Dive →

🤟

2. ClearSign Mode — Deaf, Hard-of-Hearing & No-Voice Crisis Access

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: clearsign • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + ClearSignOverlayWidget
Who it is for: Deaf, hard-of-hearing, deafblind, and non-speaking phone users, plus ASL/LSQ-first users who prefer clear plain text over dense written English.

What Clara does in ClearSign mode: Places one-tap no-voice crisis routes on every screen—SMS text (Text 988, Text HOME to 741741, Text 911), web chat (988 Chat), 711 IP relay, and ASL videophone—plus a "This device has no phone line" filter for Wi-Fi-only tablets, live visual captions/sonic radar, and a build-enforced guarantee that no feature in the app is sound-only.

👁️

3. ClearSite Mode — Blind, Low-Vision & Voice-Control Access

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: clearsite • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + ClearSiteOverlayWidget
Who it is for: Blind, low-vision, and print-disabled users, or anyone who needs the screen read aloud and operated by spoken voice commands.

What Clara does in ClearSite mode: Scans the live semantics tree so Clara announces every screen on arrival (putting crisis help first), reads every heading, button, and text field aloud with on-screen captions, executes spoken voice commands ("read the screen", "tap continue", "scroll down", "go back", "type message") with yes/no confirmation before destructive actions, and provides 7 on-device camera vision channels (text/OCR, product, currency, medicine, scene, colour, person).

🧓

4. ClearCare (ClearStep) Mode — Pre-Tap Button Reassurance, Scam Shield & Visual Comfort

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: clearcare / clearstep • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + ClearCarePanel
Who it is for: Older adults, dementia/TBI memory-support users, cautious technology users, and anyone who wants reassurance about what a button does before clicking it, protection from scams, and easy controls for size, contrast, and spacing.

What Clara does in ClearCare / ClearStep mode:

  • Pre-Tap Button Explainer ("What Things Do Before You Click Them"): Clara scans the current screen and explains in warm, plain language what each button does before you tap it—reassuring you that it will not charge money, call anyone, or break your device.
  • Scam Checker & Trust Guides: Checks suspicious phone calls, emails, and texts against 15+ elder-fraud patterns (texted 6-digit bank codes, gift cards, AnyDesk/TeamViewer remote access, fake delivery fees, grandchild bail scams).
  • Live Size, Contrast, Button/Icon Size & Spacing Controls: Instant adjustment for Text Size (1.0× to 1.65×), High-Contrast outlines & bold ink, Button & Icon Size (48dp, 56dp, 64dp), and Extra Spacing between rows and buttons.
👀

5. ClearGaze Mode — Paralysis, Eye-Tracking Dwell-to-Click & Single-Switch Access

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: cleargaze • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + ClearGazeOverlayWidget
Who it is for: Paralyzed users (ALS, quadriplegia, spinal cord injury, brainstem stroke / locked-in syndrome, cerebral palsy) who operate their device using their eyes (eye-tracking gaze hardware) or a single blink, sip-and-puff, or head switch.

What Clara does in ClearGaze mode:

  • Eye-Gaze Dwell-to-Click: Hold your gaze steady over any button for a chosen duration (0.8s, 1.2s, 1.8s, or 2.5s) to see a radial countdown ring and click automatically—zero finger or button press required.
  • Oversized Top Eye-Bar Dock: High-margin gaze targets for Scroll Up, Scroll Down, Go Back, and Rest Eyes (pauses dwell-clicking so you can read comfortably without accidental clicks).
  • Single-Switch Auto-Scanner & 48×48dp Hit Targets: Automatically steps a highlight across every interactive control on the live screen for single-switch activation, backed by UhTappable 48×48dp minimum hit targets.
🫧

6. ClearTouch Mode — Tremor Stabilization, Giant Targets & Motion-Reduction Access

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: cleartouch • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + ClearTouchOverlayWidget
Who it is for: Users with Parkinson's or essential tremor, arthritis, stroke recovery, one-handed use, or sensory/motion sensitivity who need tremor-stabilized large targets and immediate motion reduction.

What Clara does in ClearTouch mode: Filters unintentional micro-tremors and double-bounces, enlarges interactive targets for effortless one-handed tapping, and reduces visual motion one step at a time—(1) Stop the movement, (2) Soften the colours, and (3) Strip it back to words—with one-tap undo.

🧠

7. ClearFocus Mode — Dyslexia, ADHD, Brain-Fog & Reading-Ruler Access

AA: Clara (🌈) • Stackable Mode: clearfocus • SDK Hook: ClearPlusController + ClearFocusOverlayWidget
Who it is for: Users with dyslexia, ADHD, autism, post-concussion / TBI reading fatigue, long-COVID or chemo brain fog, or executive dysfunction.

What Clara does in ClearFocus mode: Provides a movable horizontal Reading Ruler (line-focus bar) that highlights 2–3 lines of text while shading distracting clutter above and below, widens letter spacing (+0.6px), word spacing (+2.0px), and line height (1.6×) across the entire app, and shows Clara's "Where Am I & What Is Next?" screen anchor.

🔌 For Other App Creators & Studios — Drop the clearplus Package Into Any App

The clearplus package sits above the host app's router/navigator (such as Flutter's MaterialApp.builder or TypeScript/React DOM overlays) with Clara as the unified AA and all 7 modes stackable on demand.

  • One AA (Clara), 7 Simultaneous Modes: Users can toggle 1, 2, 3, or all 7 modes at once via ClearPlusController while Clara handles all speech, captions, and guidance seamlessly.
  • Self-Describing UI: Every ClearPlus mode panel includes a built-in banner telling end users Who it is for and What Clara does in this mode in plain, respectful language.
