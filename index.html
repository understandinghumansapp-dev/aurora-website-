🌟 Made for Everyone Without Labels

Most technology forces human beings into rigid boxes. Aurora OS was engineered from the ground up to respect individual strengths, cognitive styles, and accessibility needs.

♾️

Neurodivergent Minds (ADHD & Autistic)

Zero cognitive overload. Parallel task trackers, visual timelines, focus modes, and direct communication without subtext or preachy moralizing.

👵👴

Older Adults & New Tech Users

Clear, calm, high-contrast interfaces. Sage companion provides step-by-step guidance, built-in scam shields, and plain English explanation with zero tech jargon.

🌈

Non-Speaking & AAC Communicators

Full-featured ClearSay symbol grids, rapid phrase composition, personalized speech synthesis, and seamless integration into daily conversation.

👁️

Blind & Low-Vision Users

ClearSite screen vision, full DOM audio narration, instant OCR reading, and seamless voice-driven computer control.

🤟

Deaf & Hard of Hearing

ClearSign descriptive audio captions, real-time ambient sound recognition (doorbells, alarms), and 100% visual parity across all OS notifications.

🦾

Motor & Switch Access

ClearTouch dwell selection, switch scanning, large hit targets, and customizable key repeat delays for motor independence.
