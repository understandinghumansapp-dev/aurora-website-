🌟 Made for Everyone Without Labels

Most technology forces human beings into rigid boxes. Aurora OS and Understanding Humans were engineered from the ground up to respect individual strengths, cognitive styles, and accessibility needs.

📣

Non-Speaking & AAC Communicators (ClearSay)

Tap-to-speak AAC symbol grids, custom emergency phrases, spoken text-to-speech output, and plain-language AI guides.

💬

Deaf & Hard of Hearing (ClearSign)

1-tap no-voice crisis routes (988 SMS, 711 TTY, VRS/ASL video relay), visual captions, and explicit honesty on app boundaries.

👁️

Blind & Low-Vision Users (ClearSite)

Full-screen audio narrator (describeScreen), interactive control listing, and hands-free spoken voice navigation.

🧓

Older Adults & Tech Safety (ClearCare)

Pre-tap button reassurance before clicking, phone/email/text scam scanner, trust guides, and live controls for text size, contrast, button size, and spacing.

👀

Paralysis, Eye-Gaze & Switch (ClearGaze)

Hands-free dwell-to-click countdown ring for eye trackers, top Eye-Bar dock, single-switch auto-scanning, and 48×48dp ClearStep hit targets.

🫧🧠

Sensory Overload & Dyslexia/ADHD (ClearTouch + ClearFocus)

1-tap sensory step-downs for autism/migraines (ClearTouch) plus a movable horizontal Reading Ruler and dyslexia spacing (ClearFocus).

♿ The 7 ClearPlus Agents + ClearStep (B2B Embeddable SDK)

Every Clear* agent tells users inside the app Who It Is For and What It Does — and can be dropped into any existing Flutter, Web, iOS, or Android app by other app companies in minutes.

View All 7 Agents & B2B SDK →
📣

1. ClearSay (Non-Verbal AAC Board)

For: Non-speaking, minimally verbal, or situational-mutism users.
Does: Tap-to-speak AAC symbol board, custom phrases, TTS, and plain-language guides.

💬

2. ClearSign (Deaf & No-Voice Crisis)

For: Deaf, hard-of-hearing, or users who cannot make a voice call.
Does: 1-tap text, chat, 711 relay, and videophone (VRS/ASL) crisis routes from every screen.

👁️

3. ClearSite (Blind & Voice Control)

For: Blind, low-vision, and eyes-free users.
Does: Reads screens aloud, lists interactive buttons, and runs hands-free spoken commands.

🧓

4. ClearCare (Elderly Reassurance & Scam Shield)

For: Older adults, seniors, and cautious tech users.
Does: Explains buttons before clicking, checks phone calls/emails/texts for scams, and adjusts text size, contrast, button size, and spacing.

👀

5. ClearGaze (Paralysis, Eye-Gaze & Switch)

For: Paralyzed users (ALS, spinal cord injury, stroke, CP) using eye trackers or switches.
Does: Dwell-to-click countdown ring, top Eye-Bar dock, and single-switch auto-scanning + ClearStep 48×48dp targets.

🫧

6. ClearTouch (Sensory Overload & Motion)

For: Autistic users, migraine sufferers, and sensory overload.
Does: Turns down 1 sensory input per tap (motion, colour, density) with instant undo.

🧠

7. ClearFocus (Dyslexia, ADHD & Reading Ruler)

For: Readers with dyslexia, ADHD, brain-fog, or post-concussion fatigue.
Does: Movable horizontal Reading Ruler spotlight band, wider letter/word/line spacing, and step anchor.
