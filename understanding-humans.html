♿ The 7 ClearPlus Universal Access Agents Built Into Understanding Humans

Every ClearPlus agent inside www.understandinghumans.app explicitly states who it is for and what it does—and can also be licensed by other app companies to drop into their already-made apps:

📣 1. ClearSay (Non-Verbal AAC Board)

For: Non-speaking, minimally speaking & AAC users

50+ symbol communication board across 6 categories, tap-to-compose phrase strip, speech output with visual fallback, and 10–12 word plain-language AI mode.

💬 2. ClearSign (Deaf & No-Voice Access)

For: Deaf, hard-of-hearing & ASL/LSQ-first users

One-tap SMS text (988, 741741, 911), web chat, 711 relay, and ASL videophone crisis routes on every screen, Wi-Fi-only filter, and zero sound-only features.

👁️ 3. ClearSite (Blind & Voice Control)

For: Blind, low-vision & print-disabled users

Live semantics screen reader that announces every screen on arrival, captions every spoken phrase, executes voice commands, and provides 7 camera description channels.

🧓 4. ClearCare (Senior Reassurance & Scam Shield)

For: Older adults & cautious tech users

Explains what buttons do before you tap them, checks phone calls/emails/texts for 15+ scam patterns with trust guides, and adjusts text size (up to 1.65×), high contrast, button/icon size (up to 64dp), and row spacing.

👀 5. ClearGaze (Paralysis & Eye-Tracking Access)

For: Paralyzed users using eye-gaze or single switches

Hands-free Eye-Gaze Dwell-to-Click countdown ring (0.8s–2.5s), oversized top Eye-Bar Dock (Scroll Up/Down, Back, Rest Eyes), single-switch auto-scanner, and 48×48dp ClearStep targets.

🫧 6. ClearTouch (Sensory & Motion Relief)

For: Sensory overload, autism, ADHD & migraine

Turns the app down one input at a time—(1) stop all animations, (2) soften colours, (3) strip back to plain words—with one-tap undo and zero diagnostic labels.

🧠 7. ClearFocus (Dyslexia, ADHD & Reading Ruler)

For: Dyslexia, ADHD, TBI & brain-fog readers

Movable horizontal Reading Ruler (line-focus bar) that shades surrounding clutter, dyslexia-friendly letter/word/line spacing, and a "Where Am I & What Is Next?" screen anchor.

🧠 Core Science & Empathy Features in Development

🔬 Neuroscience & Psychology Grounding

Evidence-based emotional frameworks that explain the "why" behind human behavior, emotional triggers, and stress responses.

🤝 Social Context Translation

Clear, direct decoding of unstated social subtext, ambiguous messages, and relationship dynamics without patronizing advice.

🌱 Compassionate Conflict Mediation

Step-by-step communication scripts and perspectives that help you express your feelings honestly and resolve interpersonal friction.

🛡️ Respectful & Private

Your emotional reflections and personal relationship logs remain private and secure on your own device.
