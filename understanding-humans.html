🧠 The Tri-Realm & Affective Lexicon™ Architecture

Built for every brain without labels, with specialized guidance engines modeled after healthy emotional dynamics:

☀️ Sol — Day Garden Guide

Structure, clarity, practical planning, progress celebration, and gently-delivered tough truths to keep you grounded.

🌙 Luna — Night Sky Terrace Guide

Warmth, emotional safety, creative reflection, and gentle validation when navigating difficult sensory and social moments.

🪐 Asteroid — Observatory Mentor

Neurodivergent analytical precision, calm pattern recognition, conflict mediation, and technical deconstruction.

📖 The Affective Lexicon™

Universal emotional vocabulary defining the subtle inner signals of human experience (Attunement, Alexithymia, Co-Regulation).
