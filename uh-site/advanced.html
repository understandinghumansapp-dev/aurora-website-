The research behind the skills

Every advanced tool is built on established research in psychology and neuroscience — taught in plain language and through play, not lectures.

"Emotions have patterns. Once you see them, they make sense."
Asteroid · your mentor
💭

Affect labeling

Putting feelings into words measurably lowers their intensity — research on affect labeling shows naming an emotion calms the brain's stress response. You'll learn to name what you feel, fast.

🎭

Emotional granularity

People who describe emotions in fine detail regulate them better (research by Lisa Feldman Barrett). You'll build a precise emotional vocabulary instead of vague ‘good’ or ‘bad.’

👀

Facial & micro-expression reading

Built on the research tradition of coding facial movement to emotion (FACS). Train your eye to catch the fast, subtle cues most people miss.

🫀

Interoception

Emotions surface in the body before the mind names them. Grounded in interoception research, you'll learn to read your own internal signals earlier.

🧠

Cognitive bias & blind spots

We all read people through bias and assumption (Kahneman & Tversky's work on heuristics). Learn to spot and correct your own blind spots for fairer, sharper reads.

🔄

Co-regulation

Nervous systems sync — calm and tension spread between people (polyvagal research). Learn how to steady a conversation instead of escalating it.

What you'll master

Skills you can actually build

  • Name emotions with precision (emotional granularity)
  • Use affect labeling to self-regulate in the moment
  • Read micro-expressions, tone, posture, and pacing
  • Map emotional patterns and predict what helps
  • Spot and correct cognitive bias and blind spots
  • De-escalate conflict and repair after rupture
  • Attune and listen actively, not reactively
  • Interpret tone and context accurately
