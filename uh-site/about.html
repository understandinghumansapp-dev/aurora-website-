Our story

Meet the founder

Understanding Humans was created by someone who knows what it feels like to be misunderstood — and chose to turn that into something that helps others.

I spent most of my life not understanding my own emotions — and realized almost no one is taught how. As a child I was scared, sad, overwhelmed. As a teenager that turned into frustration and confusion. As an adult I finally understood the truth: I didn't know my own emotional language, and no one around me did either.

No one taught us how to understand ourselves, or each other. There's no emotional lexicon in our schools, our homes, or our communities. So I built the thing I always needed — a universal emotional language, and the movement around it: Understanding Humans™.

ClearPlus™ Universal Accessibility Suite & B2B SDK

7 Built-In Accessibility Agents — For Every Body & Every Brain

Every screen in Understanding Humans™ includes our 7 self-describing ClearPlus™ agents (plus the ClearStep large-target UI primitive). Each agent clearly states Who It’s For and What It Does — and can be licensed by other app companies as a drop-in SDK for already-made apps.

📣

1. ClearSay (Non-Verbal AAC Board)

Who it’s for: Non-speaking, minimally verbal, or situational-mutism users.
What it does: Floating 📣 Say pill opens a tap-to-speak AAC symbol board, custom emergency phrases, text-to-speech output, and plain-language AI guides.

💬

2. ClearSign (Deaf & No-Voice Crisis)

Who it’s for: Deaf, hard-of-hearing, or anyone who cannot make a voice call.
What it does: Floating 💬 Sign pill gives 1-tap text (988 SMS), web chat, 711 TTY relay, and VRS/ASL videophone crisis routes from every screen.

👁

3. ClearSite (Blind & Voice Control)

Who it’s for: Blind, low-vision, and eyes-free users.
What it does: Floating 👁 Hear + 🎤 Speak pills narrate the screen aloud, list every interactive button, and run hands-free spoken commands.

🧓

4. ClearCare (Elderly Reassurance & Scam Shield)

Who it’s for: Older adults, seniors, and cautious tech users.
What it does: Explains what any button does before you click it, checks suspicious phone calls, emails, and texts for scams, and adjusts text size (1.0×–1.65×), high contrast, button/icon size, and spacing.

👀

5. ClearGaze (Paralysis, Eye-Gaze & Switch)

Who it’s for: Paralyzed users (ALS, spinal cord injury, stroke, CP) who use their eyes or a single switch.
What it does: Hands-free dwell-to-click countdown ring (0.8s–2.0s), top Eye-Bar dock, single-switch auto-scanning, and ClearStep 48×48dp hit targets.

🪧

6. ClearTouch (Sensory Overload & Motion)

Who it’s for: Autistic users, migraine sufferers, and anyone in sensory overload.
What it does: Turns the app down one sensory input per tap (motion, colour brightness, visual density) with 1-tap undo and automatic device preference matching.

🧠

7. ClearFocus (Dyslexia, ADHD & Reading Ruler)

Who it’s for: Readers with dyslexia, ADHD, brain-fog, or post-concussion fatigue.
What it does: Movable horizontal Reading Ruler spotlight band that tracks your finger/cursor, dyslexia-friendly letter/word/line spacing, and a 1-step task anchor.

🦶

+ ClearStep (48×48dp Motor Primitive)

Who it’s for: Users with hand tremors, motor fatigue, or eye-gaze pointers.
What it does: Shared UI foundation enforcing 48×48dp minimum hit targets and zero multi-step confirmation traps across all 7 ClearPlus™ overlays.

Who Kristina is

From lived experience to a movement

Kristina Green is a 33-year-old Mohawk woman from Tyendinaga Mohawk Territory — someone who has rebuilt her life with intention, clarity, and compassion. She has lived disconnection and misunderstanding, and chose to turn that experience into something that helps others.

She has watched the people she loves — autistic, ADHD, AuDHD, trauma-impacted, sensory-sensitive — struggle to express themselves or feel understood. And she realized something simple and powerful: most people aren't broken. They're misunderstood. Being different isn't a flaw — it's a way of being human.

“I've spent my life trying to understand people, and trying to be understood. I created Understanding Humans because I believe we can make connection easier — for every kind of brain.”

— Kristina Green, Founder

What guides the work

The values behind the movement

Humanity first

People are more than labels, categories, or assumptions. Everyone deserves to be understood.

Clarity over confusion

Emotions make sense when we understand them. Communication gets easier when we know what we're trying to say.

Compassion over judgment

Most people aren't trying to hurt each other — they're trying to be understood.

Connection over division

Understanding bridges differences. It brings people back together.

Science + lived experience

Emotional understanding should be grounded in research and shaped by real human experience.

Belonging for every brain

Everyone deserves to feel safe expressing themselves and listening to others.

Movement over ego. This isn't about me — it's about all of us, learning how to understand each other again.

Made to be enjoyed

Social, interactive, and genuinely fun

Learning to understand people shouldn't feel like homework. Understanding Humans is built around games, quizzes, and interactive challenges — with social features that let you learn alongside family, friends, classmates, or teammates. You build real skills by playing, together.
